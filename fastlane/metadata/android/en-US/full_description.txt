Sauve is an open-source first aid and vital emergency app, designed to guide you quickly and accurately when every second counts.

Free, open-source (MPL-2.0), ad-free, account-free, and 100% offline: it does not even ask for Internet permission. It works instantly everywhere, even in dead zones, undergrounds, or remote outdoor trails.

THE 4 VITAL EMERGENCIES STEP BY STEP
• Cardiac Arrest: Express responsiveness and breathing diagnosis, direct emergency dispatch (112 / 911), 110 bpm metronome given by a sound click and by vibration (the sound can be switched off) so you can keep both hands and eyes on the victim, vocal compression count guidance (continuous or 30:2 ratio), rescuer rotation reminder every 2 minutes, and AED operation guide.
• Severe Choking: Immediate detection of partial vs total airway obstruction, guided back blows then abdominal thrusts (Heimlich maneuver), with variants for infants, pregnant and obese people, and one-tap switch to CPR in case of unconsciousness.
• Severe Bleeding: Immediate direct pressure, emergency tourniquet application with automatic timestamp logging and medical handover instructions.
• Unconsciousness / Recovery Position: Airway check, illustrated step-by-step Recovery Position (PLS), and continuous breathing monitoring.

DIRECT EMERGENCY CALLS
Quick one-tap access to emergency numbers adapted to European and French frameworks (15 SAMU, 18 Fire, 112 European Emergency, 114 SMS for deaf and hard-of-hearing).

EVIDENCE-BASED GUIDELINES
All protocols are based on international recommendations from the European Resuscitation Council (ERC 2025) and the French national first aid reference PSC (July 2026 edition). The content was checked against the published PSC recommendations, but it has not yet been validated by an instructor or a physician. Sauve is a memory aid: it replaces neither training nor calling for help.

ZERO DATA COLLECTION
Your phone is your personal intervention tool. No tracking, no user account, no analytics. Sauve does not replace certified first aid training, but gives you clear guidance when the unexpected occurs.
